# Live-process forensics

1. Confirm the process and diagnostic access are available; do not restart, modify, or attach invasive probes without approval.
2. Capture the smallest relevant logs, counters, stack, or profile needed to test the task's hypothesis.
3. Separate observed facts from possible causes and note the command, time, and environment.
4. Return the diagnosis as evidence for the parent to decide the next task; do not turn an investigation into an unapproved repair.
